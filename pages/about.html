About this demonstration

D·TELCO is a telecom marketplace built on Dengage, shown to telecom operators. It is not a business and nothing here is for sale.

Every product, price, subscriber and message is invented unless a real operator published it, and the ones that were published carry the same numerals in dollars. Anything invented is marked demo data in the catalogue itself, so it can never quietly become a claim.

Every experience here maps to a documented Dengage capability, and each one was confirmed in the account before it was shown. Where a capability is best seen in the panel itself, it is shown there and said so plainly.
